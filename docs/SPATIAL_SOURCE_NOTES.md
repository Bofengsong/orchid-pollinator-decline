# Spatial source notes

The repository contains processed spatial inputs used by the supplied scripts,
including the global orchid-hotspot layer, 1-degree environmental-risk layers,
and the final masked WCVP lineage grid.

The full raw environmental workflow relied on large external source products
that are not duplicated in this compact private review repository:

- PESTCHEMGRIDS v2.01 pesticide-pressure products;
- TerraClimate annual mean temperature and climatic water-deficit products;
- Global Human Modification habitat-pressure products;
- World Checklist of Vascular Plants (WCVP) distribution and taxonomic products.

The processed files included here are sufficient to reproduce the submitted
priority and lineage-map figures from the locked analysis state. Public release
metadata should include the relevant source citations, versions, licences and
access dates.
